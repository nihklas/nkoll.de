I'm a software developer who builds mostly web-applications and teaches
himself Zig and low-level programming in his free-time. I have a few
small contributions to open source projects, which i plan to increase in
the future, as well as some notable projects done completely by myself.

I came to love the linux/unix terminal environment (and use neovim btw),
so most of my worktime is done on the command-line. I am very
comfortable with hopping around on different servers and prefer using a
VPS with SSH instead of hosting services. I also have a little Raspberry
Pi sitting in my room as a home server managing my army of little
microcontrollers.

## Interests

- bash/CLI
- Self-hosting
- Backend Development
- Low-level Development
