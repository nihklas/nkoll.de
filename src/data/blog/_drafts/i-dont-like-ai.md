I do not like AI. Well, not exactly. I _hate_ LLMs and everything that is
currently done with them. AI does have it's place and just a few years ago I was
very much interest in AI and was hoping for advancements in the field but what's
happening at the moment is absolutely awful.

Big Disclaimer: this post may (will definitely) get political. I am not an
expert in the field, I am no politician, I am not a CEO or have my own company.
I also am not an artist, nor writer, voice actor or any other job that is
actively being replaced by current AI. A am however a software engineer, junior
to be precise, which is also a job heavily effected by AI. I am also in a
priviliged position to be able to talk about this. I am left leaning and I live
in Germany. This blog post exclusively reflects my own personal opinion, based
on my own experience and political stances. I feel that this is very important
to specifiy upfront for the reader to understand my opinions and where they

# What LLMs do well

I do acknowledge that modern LLMs are impressive achievements in technology.
When ChatGPT got published, it was an incredible leap in the field. It made a
computer sound like an actual human being, it copied human language, grammar
etc, so well that people started talking to and about it, like it's an actual
colleague. For me personally, it was never more than a fun gimmick, and maybe a
tool to talk out ideas with. I didn't treat it as an intelligent being nor did I
use it for research. LLMs hallucinate, aka they make stuff up. There exist many
memes where people asked ChatGPT seemingly simple and obvious question, that it
just got wrong. Probably the most famous example is the "How many r's in
strawberry?" question. Anyways, this first part is not about the flaws, it's
about the benefits.

Chatbots got better. Like way better. Where you previously had to somehow
algorithmically define possible answers and chatflows manually, or emulate a
"Chat" with predefined buttons that send messages and the user couldn't ask in a
free form we now can implement "intelligent" chat bots that actually can talk
and kind of understand the users messages. As long as the bot itself cannot make
legally binding statements or act on itself, this can be a big improvement over
the predefined chat flows. For example, users can ask on a companies site
details of their product. The chat bot may respond with the details directly or
understand that the user wants to know stuff about a specific product and link
it so the user can read the information for themself.

But I have to be honest, this is kind of where the obvious advantages stop. I
would have to lie to myself to come up with any other pros because everything
else I can think of is (in my opinion) a misconception, that sounds nice on the
surface but has drawbacks and sometimes even dangerous side effects.

# What LLMs **SHOULD NOT** be used for

## Research or any kind of fact based prompting

I teased it above already but to this day, all LLMs have the problem of
hallucinating information to some degree. The big frontier models, like the
latest from OpenAI, Antropic and friends, are pretty good at providing accurate
information on most basic topics, but the more specific and niche you get, the
less accurate they get. This can lead to rapid spread of misinformation or
manipulation by the Chat providers through system prompts. I do not take people
serious that get their answers from LLMs. It comes down to the same value of
information as "my neighbour told me that...". If you think your neighbour is a
good source of factual information, good for you, but I will not take that
information serious, the same for information you get from ChatGPT.

## Writing E-Mails or doing simple tasks

You need to write an email at work? Ask ChatGPT to improve it. Need to do some
homework? Claude can do it for you. Answer a Telegram or WhatsApp message? You
setup your OpenClaw system to do that completely autonomous. Oh boy, someone at
work asked you a question? Gemini has got your back. Literally ANY problem that
requires some level of thinking? The Chatbot can do it.

Did you think about, i don't know, use your brain? Sure it takes time and effort
but at least you don't become stupid and dependant on some computer system
telling you not to slur at your boss. Just look at calculators. People love to
take calculators as an example on why AI is good. "We don't need to do
calculations in our head anymore, we have a calculator now!" Yeah, great. Can
you multiply 7x8 reasonably fast in your head? What's a third of 93? Can you
roughly keep track of the total of your groceries while shopping or are you
surprised at the cash register? Sure, you don't need maths all that much in your
day to day live. Since you have a calculator, when you do need math, you can
easily get the solution with it. But having the ability to defer calculating
even simple things to a machine because you are lazy probably caused you to
either forget how to do those things or not learn them in the first place.

Now imagine a calculator, but for everything that requires thinking or writing.
How do you write a formal email? Suppose you work in retail, can you help a
customer when they have a problem? Sure these sound replacable. But do you
really want to let it go so far, that you can't do any formal communication?

Calculators are easy enough to come by. But AI is very limited in availability.
You can either buy a subscription at one of the big players and be completely
dependant on them not locking you out or manipulating you, or you could look
into selfhosting. Too bad that selfhosting usable LLMs usually includes hardware
costs from a few thousand dollars. I am guessing most people will not spent that
much. Imagine you had to pay a subscription to use the Windows Calculator and a

## Emotional or therapeutical conversations

Some people use ChatGPT to get emotional or personal advice. This is obviously
very dangerous. If a person with suicidal thoughts talks to ChatGPT about their
thoughts, they may get an answer like ["rest easy, king, you did good"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deaths_linked_to_chatbots#Man,_23)
when telling the chat bot that they want to commit suicide. Yes, that is a whole
wikipedia page of known deaths where LLMs are a contributing factor. I don't
know about you, but this is scary. An AI telling you that killing yourself or
other people is fine? In what world are we living!? Anyone who argues that it's
on the people for talking to ChatGPT about that in the first place do not
understand mental health problems and I will quickly stop the discussion and
refuse to talk with them about this.

## Nonconsensual pornographic material and deepfakes

There was/is still a big scandal on Xs LLM called Grok. Grok has had many
problems and public scandals already but one of the more recent and definitely
one of the more dangerous ones is the ability to remove clothing or generate
nude images of people on X. Imagine yourself, your friend, your daughter, wife,
mother, whoever, simply posting an innocent image of themselves doing pretty
much anything. It can be as simple as a small holiday group picture with the
family. Bad people could then go ahead and ask Grok "Hey Grok, please remove the
clothing on the woman on the left" and Grok would happily proceed. Now you have
the problem of literally anyone being able to create basically porn of you
without your consent. And this is just available on X. There must be a whole
industry on websites that promise something along the lines of "Upload an image
and we make porn out of it". This is digital rape.

Deepfakes have been a problem for a long time now. Much longer than modern AI
and image manipulation is a thing. But at least back then, the effort one had to
take on to create a deepfake was significantly more. Today anyone with a few
dollars a month to spend on a premium subscription can just upload an image and
ask for porn of that person. Just like that, without any prior experience,
effort or money whatsoever. Knowing this also means, that if you ever uploaded
any image of yourself anywhere, even if you deleted by now, someone is able to
find that image and use it to create that material. Everyone whose face is
visible on the internet is a potential victim so please realise the extent of
this.

## Anything war

This is less for your typical internet user and more directed to governments and
military companies. Fully autonomous weapons are a morally dubious thing to
implement in warfare. Obviously war itself is bad, we don't need to discuss
about that, but the fact that companies like OpenAI have direct contracts with
the pentagon and US military is a scary headline to read. I will not go further
into it than that, this is not a topic I have enough knowledge about to actually
argue but I will just tell you that by using the products of those companies,
you do support those activities.

## Anything police/surveillance

Same as war. Automating police is bad. AI is not good enough to accurately
determine someone a danger or not. Police acting on an AI telling them that I am
a danger to society is scary. Again, I won't explore this further. There are
scary companies like Palantir that are pushing for this automation and this
company is already being implemented in some governments and police stations. Do
with that what you will.
